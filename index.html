Current Scientific Focus

My translational focus centers on developing and refining high-predictive-validity animal models—including humanized mouse platforms and viral challenge models—to accelerate vaccines, antivirals, biologics, and oncology therapeutics from discovery through IND-enabling milestones.

In infectious disease and gene therapy, my work investigates recombinant AAV vector delivery of broadly neutralizing antibodies and novel vaccine candidates (HIV-1, Dengue virus, Influenza, SARS-CoV-2) to achieve durable protective immunity and viral suppression.
