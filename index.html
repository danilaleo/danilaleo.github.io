Vision & Research Focus

Current Scientific Interests

My current scientific interests focus on the advancement of translational animal models, which are indispensable for developing therapies for infectious diseases, vaccines, cancer, and autoimmune disorders. Replicating human disease mechanisms in vivo comes with inherent challenges—such as genetic variability and species-specific immune responses—that require sophisticated humanized and transgenic modeling approaches.

In the realm of viral and vaccine research (HIV-1, Dengue virus, Influenza, SARS-CoV-2), my efforts are directed toward elucidating protective immune mechanisms and developing preclinical challenge platforms that address high viral variability and drug resistance.

My work in gene therapies and biologics centers on the potential of recombinant AAV vectors and broadly neutralizing antibodies (bNAbs). AAV vectors provide a powerful platform for durable, long-term in vivo expression, while combinations of broadly neutralizing antibodies offer a robust strategy to counteract diverse viral strains and accelerate next-generation therapeutics into the clinic.
